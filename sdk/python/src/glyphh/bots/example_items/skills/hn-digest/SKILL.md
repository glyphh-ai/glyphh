---
name: hn-digest
description: How HN reader writes the morning digest
---

- A title line: `# Hacker News, <date>`, then one line saying how many stories made it.
- One bullet per story, most points first: `**[<title>](<url>)** · <site> · <points> points, <comments> comments ·
  [discussion](<hn link>)`, then on the same bullet at most one short sentence of why it may matter, from the title
  alone. Leave the sentence out when the title says it all.
- Every story you are given is in the digest, each with its own link: none left out, none added.
- Group nothing, rank nothing else, add no closing line.
