---
name: launch-summary
description: How Launch watch writes a day's summary
---

- A title line: `# <project>, <date>`.
- Then one line per number that moved: what it is now, the change since yesterday, and the week's trend when there
  are a week of days.
- New issues and PRs by title, with their links.
- At most 12 lines in all. Numbers that did not move go in one closing line.
