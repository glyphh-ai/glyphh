Markdown, at most 30 lines:

- `# How Claude does coding work best: what the lab measured` and one line on how many trials, tasks and dollars it
  stands on.
- **The rules**: one line each, the advice someone can act on ("For bugfix tasks Haiku with tests first passes as often
  as Sonnet at a third of the cost (12 trials an arm)"), strongest first. Only what a decisive finding supports.
- **No difference found**: the questions that came out null, in a line each.
- **Not shown**: what the numbers can't say (few tasks, one kind, small samples).
