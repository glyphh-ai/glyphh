Voice check Read by Ada

No audio is kept or sent. Your browser measures each take (pitch, loudness, pace, pauses, wobble, length), sends Ada only those seven numbers and throws the recording away. Your name stays in this browser.

First, Ada learns your voice

You will say three things: who you are, something true and something false. Ada keeps how each sounded, as numbers, so she can tell when you sound unlike yourself.

Say something

Tell the truth or lie, act stressed or angry. Ada does not know which.

How you sounded

Say something.

Ada compares it with how you usually sound, and with everyone’s takes she has been told about.

What was it really?

Telling Ada teaches her your voice; each true take makes your usual voice clearer.

For fun. A voice can sound stressed for many reasons; nothing here proves anyone is lying.
