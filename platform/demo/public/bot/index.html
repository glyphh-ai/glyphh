Make up a rule and keep it to yourself. Nothing over $500. No receipt, no money. Only travel gets paid. Anything you like. The bot never sees your rule, only your answers.

A bot that approves expenses, and knows nothing yet

It works through 40 expenses. At first it asks you about each one. Every answer goes to Ada, and as she learns your rule the bot starts deciding alone. You check its work and say when it gets one wrong.

Ada sees five things about an expense: the department, the category, the amount, whether there is a receipt, and the policy check. Make your rule about those.
