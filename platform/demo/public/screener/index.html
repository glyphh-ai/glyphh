How close it is

Two numbers: how alike the nearest fake is, and how alike the nearest real one is, from 0 (nothing shared) to 1 (the same). Then Ada's own decision, its confidence, and its fact tree.

What this is, and is not

  • One Ada model holds two sets of resumes read the same way, section by section, words only: fakes and real ones. The whole model, every role and weight.
  • The fakes are generated, fictional applications: a program wrote each one. The nearest fake is named by its made-up name and role. A real one is only ever shown by its source and category, never a name.
  • It says how alike the words are to what the model holds. It is not proof that anyone is real or fake. What was checked, and what it showed.
