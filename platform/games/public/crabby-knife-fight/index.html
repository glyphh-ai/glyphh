How to play

Run. Jump twice. Cut three times.

01

Double jump

Jump, then jump again in the air. Hold it to go higher. In water, jump is a stroke upward.

02

Dash

A burst that leaves pink ghosts. Nothing hurts him in a dash.

03

Three cuts, then the spin

Three in a row. The third is a spin that hits both sides, twice as hard.

04

The stab down

In the air, down and slash. It hits harder and bounces him off what it hits.

05

Five hearts

Lose them and you are back at the last flag; in a boss fight, at the door of his arena, and he is whole again.

06

Keep moving

Stand still for a few seconds and something comes down on you: a gull, a pigeon in the city, a frying pan in the kitchen.

On a keyboard

Move with the arrows or A and D. Jump with Space, Up, W or Z. Slash with X, J or K. Dash with C, L or Shift. Hold Down and slash in the air to stab down; hold Down and jump to drop through a ledge. Esc goes back to the map.

On a phone

A pad for thumbs lies over the game: left, down and right under one thumb; DASH, SLASH and a big JUMP under the other.

What is in it

Seven places. Seven traitors. Five knives.

A true side-on platformer, and every place is long: seven or eight checkpoint flags, its own way through, its own things that hurt, and a knife fight at the end. Clear one to open the next.

Seven places

The dock, the park, the playground, the city (over the traffic, and under the street in the sewer), the fish market, the truck on the highway, and the restaurant: the kitchen, and the pot.

Seven bosses

A different kind of crab every time, each with his own attacks: Captain Pinch, Old Barnacle, Fiddler Fitz, Longlegs Lou, The Duchess, Coco the Crusher and King Kraw. A red ! goes up before every attack.

Treasure

Coins along the way, cut gems on the side ledges, diamonds in the hard places, and chests to cut open. It all counts as gold: a coin is 1, a gem 5, a diamond 20.

Five knives

He starts with the Rusty Bait Knife. The Fillet Knife, The Sawtooth, The Golden Claw and Kraken's Tooth are bought on the map with gold, 150 to 1400. Each reaches farther and cuts harder.

The band

Loud, and made as you play: drums, a bass, an overdriven guitar and a lead, with no sound files. A fast track for every place and a faster one for every boss. Two sliders set the music and the effects.

Fastest times

Clear a place and your time goes on its list, under the name you choose. Times are as each player's browser reported them: read them as claims, not proofs.

Claude

How the crab connects to Claude.

Crabby Knife Fight is also a connector for Claude. Add it, describe a place, and Claude writes a small spec; the game checks it and builds it from its own parts. Each made place gets a link to share, its own fastest times, and a spot on the MADE PLACES list in the game.

  1. In Claude, open Settings, Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Crabby Knife Fight and paste its address:
    https://crabbyknifefight.com/mcp
  3. Press Connect and sign in with the account you play with.
  4. Start a chat with the connector switched on, and describe a place.

In Claude Code

One line adds the same connector:

claude mcp add --transport http crabby https://crabbyknifefight.com/mcp

What a place is made of

A look, any of the seven. 3 to 24 stretches in the order you want: open runs, gaps, a tower, a cave, a gauntlet, lanes of rolling things, springs, a climb up, a drop down, a pool. A boss copied from one of the seven, with your name for him. Coins, gems, diamonds, chests, hearts, villains, ledges and springs where you want them. Up to 40 places a player.
