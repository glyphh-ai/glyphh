How to play

American mah jongg, played properly.

Four players, 152 tiles, the Charleston, a card of hands, calls and jokers. It is not Hong Kong or Chinese mah jongg, which is a different game.

01

Come in

Sign in once. Your character, your brags and your place on the ladder are kept with your account, on every device.

02

Dress your table

Pick the room, the mat, the tiles and the brags you play for. Change them whenever you like.

03

The Charleston

Right, across, left; a second one only if all four say yes; then a courtesy pass of up to three.

04

Calls and jokers

Call a thrown tile for a pung, a kong, a quint or mah jongg, and take a joker from anyone's shown set when you hold the real tile.

05

The card

The game's own, The First Card: 36 hands in nine families. It is always a tap away, the hands you are nearest to first.

06

New to the game?

Learn to play is nine short pages with the real tiles, then a practice hand against the computer on Easy.

Bams & Brags plays from its own card of hands and is not affiliated with the National Mah Jongg League.

What is in it

The whole thing is about style.

Six rooms, eleven mats, ten sets of tiles and four sets of brags, and every one of them is yours to play with from the first hand.

Rooms, mats and tiles

The Country Club in peonies tonight, the Rooftop in black and gold tomorrow. Eleven mats, each with its own art, and ten sets of tiles.

Brags

Each time you make mah jongg you win a brag: a little charm set down beside you, so the whole table can see how your night is going. Four sets, six charms in each.

Achievements

912 of them. Everything you do at the table counts, each count climbs a ladder of twelve creatures, from Chick to Dragon, and everyone who plays is ranked on the ladder.

Your friends, your table

Host a game and send the link, find a game with a chair free, or play now against the computer at Easy, Regular, Hard or Impossible. The computer takes any empty chair.

Tournaments

Host one in a minute: everyone is seated four to a table, every table is dealt the same hands, and the standings are live. Hosting and playing in them is what the pass is for; anyone may watch.

Table talk

What is said and what happens, beside the table. Say it quickly or in your own words, and tap anyone to see who she is, her brags and the table she keeps.

New: leagues

Leagues, each with a site of its own.

Start a league for your club, your neighbourhood or your regular game. It gets its own site at bamsandbrags.com/leagues/your-league, dressed from the game's own rooms, mats and tiles in any of 38 templates; members you invite or let in; a season table; and online tournaments your members play without the pass.

Events in person

A page of its own, registration free or paid, and a ticket with a code for everyone who registers.

At the door

Check people in on an iPad at the door, or let them check in on their own phones.

Seating and scoring

Seat the rounds in tables of four, and score every hand live, each one confirmed by a second person.

The feed and the board

Everyone posts photos to the event's feed, and the standings and the tables go up on a TV as a live board.

A hand from a picture

The pictures show a staged example, not a real event. Registration fees are paid to the league; the game keeps 5% of each paid registration.

Claude

How the table connects to Claude.

Bams & Brags is also a connector for Claude. With the pass you can ask for tiles, a mat, a room or a set of brags of your own, and they are on your shelf in the game the next time you look. Claude can also make your character from a photo you show it, host a tournament for you, and open a hand to play in the chat. A league's tools need no pass.

  1. In Claude, open Settings, Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Bams & Brags and paste its address:
    https://bamsandbrags.com/mcp
  3. Press Connect and sign in with the account you play with.
  4. Start a chat with the connector switched on, and ask.

Make me a tile set in blush and gold with deco numerals.

A mat in navy with gold cranes.

A set of brags about my book club.

In Claude Code

One line adds the same connector:

claude mcp add --transport http bams https://bamsandbrags.com/mcp

What you make is yours alone. Brags you make are shown to other players only after you ask and we have looked at them.
