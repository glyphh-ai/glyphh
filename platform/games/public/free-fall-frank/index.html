How to play

Two buttons. Count your taps.

01

Tap left or right

Frank is three blocks tall. Each tap tumbles him a quarter turn that way, so he lies down, stands up and moves across.

02

Fit the gap

Every floor has a gap. Get there the right way up, in the right place, not too early and not too late. One touch and he is back at the top.

03

Stay on the beat

The gates land on the beat of the track. Posts punish an early tap, springs fling him, blowers shove him, saws swing in time.

04

Four difficulties

Easy sends him back only to the start of the stretch. Hard is 20% faster, insane 40% faster, and scores count for more.

05

Coins and Franks

Three Franks are free. Coins you bank on the way down earn the others, or all of them unlock at once for $0.99.

06

Leaderboards and clips

Every level has its own leaderboard. Record your fall, and share the clip or your score card when you clear a level.

Claude

How Frank connects to Claude.

Free Fall Frank is also a connector for Claude. Add it once, and Claude can build levels and Franks for you, publish them with a link, read the leaderboards, and open a level to play right in the chat.

  1. In Claude, open Settings, Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Free Fall Frank and paste its address:
    https://freefallfrank.com/mcp
  3. Press Connect and sign in with Google. That makes what you build yours, on every device.
  4. Start a chat with the connector switched on, and ask for a level.

In Claude Code

One line adds the same connector:

claude mcp add --transport http frank https://freefallfrank.com/mcp

No Google account?

In the game, open settings and Profile, and copy your own connector address: the same address with your code on the end. Add that one and there is nothing to sign in to.

Build a level

Say what you want. Claude builds it.

You describe the level in your own words. Claude shows you what it will be like, changes it as you say, and publishes it when you are happy. Every level can be finished: the game checks.

Make me a Free Fall Frank level called Lava Run: about a minute, starts easy, a maze in the middle and a brutal finish.

Build a level for my little sister: slow, lots of coins, in the candy world.

A level made only of patterns, left-left-right-right, getting faster each time.

  1. Ask. Claude drafts the level and previews it: how long, how many gates and taps, how many coins.
  2. Change it. Harder, shorter, another world, a different ending. Say it and Claude builds it again.
  3. Publish. You get a link like freefallfrank.com/?level=abc1234. It is free to play and has its own leaderboard.
  4. Go public. Ask Claude to submit it, or press Go public on your profile. We look at each level, then every player can find it.
