Org: review

Where an org admin (Sam Rivera, for Northwind) reads what members want to publish and decides. The queue by status, searchable and paged; a listing’s detail with its preview, screenshots, what it needs and what it can do, the changes since the last version, and its author; then approve, request changes or reject. Marketplace listings go on to Glyphh, whose review shows here read only. Desktop 1200, phone 390.
