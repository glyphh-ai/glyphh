The left rail · 1 of 2

The menu moves from the top bar to a rail on the left that collapses to icons. You, your org and everything about your account sit at the bottom left. Org admins get an Org group and super admins a Glyphh admin group, each opening to its pages. On a phone the same rail is a drawer from the left. Icons are Lucide (approved by Chris, 2026-10-12), stroke 2, 20 px. The page area is a stand-in: every app board's content sits there unchanged.
