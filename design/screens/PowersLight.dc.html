Connections and what they may do

Powers sit beside Loops: everything a super power can use. A connection is always your own account; Pipedream holds it and its sign-in, Glyphh stores no secret. Connect from the catalog, sign in on Pipedream's own page inside a modal (or its own window when a provider won't open in a frame), then set each tool to Allow, Ask or Deny. A tool with no setting asks; in a run nobody is watching, Ask is refused. Grouped by risk, as in server-old: Read, Create and update, Delete, Other.
