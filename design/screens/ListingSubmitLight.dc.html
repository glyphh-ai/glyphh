Publish a power or super power (light) · 1 of 4

A member lists something they made: for Northwind only, or for everyone in the Glyphh marketplace. The editor takes the icon (a super power wears its cat), name, a one-line summary, description, category, screenshots, version and what’s new; what it needs is read from what it uses. Submitting sends it to the org admin, and marketplace listings on to Glyphh. Every step shows where it is and who has it; changes requested come back as notes on the fields. Desktop 1200, phone 390.
