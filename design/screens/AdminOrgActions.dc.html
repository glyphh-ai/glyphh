Org actions and plans

Only Glyphh's own staff (super admins, users of the parent org) see Glyphh admin in the top nav, with Orgs, Models, Marketplace and Plans under it. Orgs lists every org with its plan, seats, machines, credit balance, spend and status; opening one shows its overview, members, machines, powers, super powers and billing, and the actions only staff have: gift credit (dollars, with a note, in the org's ledger), change or comp its plan, and disable it. Plans holds each plan family and its versions: a change is a new version, sold to new orgs, while every org keeps the version it is on, at its price, until it changes plan or is moved with notice. It also holds the billing settings: what a connection call costs per started 30 seconds and the smallest and largest top-up, each change kept with who made it and from when. Credit is dollars. Desktop 1200 × 1120, phone 390 × 1460.
