Keys for scripts, CI and CLIs that would rather send a key than sign in; Claude, Codex and machines sign in their own way. A member finds their keys in the account menu: each key's name, its prefix to tell it apart, when it was made, last used and expires, and its status. A key is shown once, when made: Glyphh stores only its hash. Org admins decide whether members may make their own keys, and see and revoke every key in the org under Org › API keys.
