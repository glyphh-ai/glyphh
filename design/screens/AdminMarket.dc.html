Glyphh admin: the marketplace

Where Glyphh decides what goes on the marketplace. Orgs send listings once their org admin approves them; Glyphh sends its own. Each one is checked first by Glyphh's own reviewer super power, Marketplace Reviewer, which writes a report: what the listing asks for against what it does, its dependencies, its screenshots, its description, secrets in the bundle, and a run in a sandbox. Then a person decides: publish, request changes (with a note per field, the agent's findings in one click) or reject with a reason. Every listing carries its version (semver, always higher than the last, never reused) and its release notes (required, up to 2,000 characters); reviewers see both, and every earlier version keeps its own. Icons are square 512 px PNGs; screenshots are PNG or JPEG, up to six, 5 MB each; never SVG. Every word between Glyphh and the org lands in the listing's thread. Published listings are managed here too: feature, change category, unpublish, versions; and the categories and the featured row themselves. Desktop 1200 wide, phone 390 wide.
