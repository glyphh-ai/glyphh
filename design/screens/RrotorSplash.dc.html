The splash

The first thing every tab on a machine shows, before its desktop. It says at once that you are on a machine and which one: its name is the headline, with where it runs, what it is and its own address. While it starts it lists what it is doing in plain words. The machine's name, address and account stay in the bar at the bottom, and carry on into the desktop. Desktop 1200 × 760, phone 390 × 844. Motion: the ring's comet, the blink and the steps' spinners; all still when motion is off.
