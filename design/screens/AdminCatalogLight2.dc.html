Models and providers (light) · 2 of 4

The catalog everyone picks models from: about 3,900 models across 125 providers. It is Glyphh's reference data, the same for every org; anyone signed in reads it, only super admins change it. Two tables: providers (name, logo, how to reach it) and catalog models (the provider's model id, a name, a description, a type). It holds no keys, prices or limits. A super admin can turn a provider or a model off, or remove it. Off is for now, the org limits' rule: members' powers on it are disabled with the reason and come back, untouched, when it is on again. Removed is for good: those powers can only be deleted, so removing asks you to type the name. The old list came without descriptions, so writing them is a job of its own. Providers without artwork on this board (DeepSeek, Cohere, Cerebras, …) show the LogoTile's initials; in the build a provider shows its uploaded mark. Desktop 1200, phone 390.
